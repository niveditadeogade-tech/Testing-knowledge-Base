3. APPOINTMENTS
=====================================================
Q: How do I book an appointment?
A: By phone at +91 98765 43210, on WhatsApp at the same number, or through this voice assistant. We need the patient's full name, mobile number, preferred doctor or department, and preferred date and time.

Q: How long is a consultation slot?
A: General consultations are 15 minutes. First visits for Cardiology, Gynaecology and Endocrinology are 30 minutes. Physiotherapy sessions are 45 minutes. Dental check-ups are 20 minutes.

Q: Can I walk in without an appointment?
A: Yes, walk-ins are seen when the doctor has a gap, but waiting can be 45 minutes or more. Appointments are always seen first.

Q: What is the consultation fee?
A: General Medicine Rs. 500. Paediatrics Rs. 600. Gynaecology Rs. 800. Cardiology Rs. 1,000. Orthopaedics Rs. 800. Dermatology Rs. 700. ENT Rs. 700. Endocrinology Rs. 900. Dental check-up Rs. 400. Physiotherapy session Rs. 600. A follow-up with the same doctor within 7 days of the first visit is free.

Q: Can I cancel or reschedule an appointment?
A: Yes. Cancel or reschedule at least 2 hours before the slot by phone, WhatsApp or this assistant, and there is no charge. Cancelling later than that, or not turning up, is recorded as a no-show; three no-shows in 60 days means the next booking needs an advance payment of Rs. 200.

Q: What if the doctor is unavailable on the day?
A: We call or message you as soon as we know, and offer the next free slot with the same doctor or another doctor in the same department. If you pre-paid, the full amount is refunded within 5 to 7 working days.

Q: Do you offer video consultations?
A: Yes, for General Medicine, Paediatrics, Dermatology and Endocrinology follow-ups. The fee is the same as an in-clinic visit. A video link is sent by WhatsApp 10 minutes before the slot.

Q: What should I bring to my first visit?
A: A photo ID, previous prescriptions and test reports, the list of medicines you take, and your insurance card if you have one. Please arrive 10 minutes early for registration.

4. DIAGNOSTIC LAB AND SCANS
=====================================================
Q: What are the lab timings?
A: Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Sunday, 9:00 AM to 12:00 noon. Sample collection at home is available daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Q: Do I need to fast before a blood test?
A: For fasting blood sugar and lipid profile, fast for 8 to 10 hours; water is allowed. For HbA1c, CBC, thyroid profile, vitamin D and vitamin B12, no fasting is needed.

Q: What are the prices of common tests?
A: CBC Rs. 300. Fasting blood sugar Rs. 100. HbA1c Rs. 550. Lipid profile Rs. 600. Thyroid profile (T3, T4, TSH) Rs. 700. Liver function test Rs. 650. Kidney function test Rs. 650. Vitamin D Rs. 1,100. ECG Rs. 300. Chest X-ray Rs. 450. Ultrasound abdomen Rs. 1,200. Full Body Checkup Basic Rs. 1,999. Full Body Checkup Advanced Rs. 3,999.

Q: When will my reports be ready?
A: Blood tests: same day by 6:00 PM for samples given before 11:00 AM; otherwise next morning. X-ray and ECG: within 30 minutes. Ultrasound: same day. Reports are sent by WhatsApp and email, and printed copies can be collected at the lab counter.

Q: Is there a charge for home sample collection?
A: Rs. 100 within 10 km of the clinic. It is free if the total bill is Rs. 1,500 or more.

5. PHARMACY
=====================================================
Q: Is there a pharmacy?
A: Yes, on the ground floor of Lotus Towers, open daily 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Home delivery is free within 5 km for orders above Rs. 300.

Q: Can I order medicines from a previous prescription?
A: Only prescriptions issued by our doctors in the last 6 months can be refilled. Schedule H medicines need a valid prescription. Controlled drugs cannot be home delivered.

6. INSURANCE, PAYMENTS AND BILLING
=====================================================
Q: Which insurance companies do you accept?
A: Cashless is available with Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Niva Bupa and Care Health. Other insurers: pay first and claim reimbursement; we provide all bills and reports.

Q: Does cashless apply to consultations?
A: Cashless applies to day-care procedures and admissions. Most consultation-only visits are paid at the clinic, depending on your policy's OPD cover.

Q: Which payment methods do you accept?
A: Cash, UPI, all major debit and credit cards, and net banking. EMI is available on bills above Rs. 10,000.

Q: How do I get a GST or tax invoice?
A: Tell the billing counter when you pay; invoices are also emailed on request. Healthcare services are generally exempt from GST; pharmacy items carry the applicable GST.

Q: How can I get a refund?
A: Refunds for cancelled pre-paid appointments or tests go to the original payment method in 5 to 7 working days.

=====================================================
7. PREPARATION AND AFTER-CARE
=====================================================
Q: How should I prepare for an ECG or a cardiology visit?
A: Wear loose clothing, avoid heavy meals and caffeine for 2 hours before, and bring all your current medicines.

Q: How should I prepare for an abdominal ultrasound?
A: Fast for 6 hours and drink 4 to 5 glasses of water an hour before the test; do not pass urine until the scan is done.

Q: What should I do after a dental procedure?
A: Do not eat or drink for 2 hours if you had local anaesthesia, avoid very hot food for 24 hours, take medicines as prescribed, and call us if bleeding continues for more than 30 minutes or you have fever.

Q: What are the vaccination services?
A: Childhood vaccines as per the national schedule, flu vaccine, hepatitis B, typhoid, HPV and travel vaccines. Book with Paediatrics for children and General Medicine for adults. Please bring the vaccination card.

=====================================================
- The assistant books, cancels and reschedules appointments and answers questions from this document only.
- It must never diagnose, prescribe or suggest medicine doses. For medical advice it says: "I can't give medical advice, but I can book you with the right doctor."

- If it does not know the answer, it offers to take the caller's name and number so reception can call back within 2 hours during clinic timings.
- It confirms the patient's name, doctor, date and time before ending every booking call.
